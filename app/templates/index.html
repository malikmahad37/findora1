Safety Guidelines for Returning Belongings

Please follow these common-sense safety practices when meeting to return or claim items:

1. Meet in daylight in public

Choose busy locations like campus main gates, library entrances, metro stations, or popular cafes. Never meet in private residences or secluded spots.

2. Verify ownership details

Ask the claimant for a private detail only the owner knows (e.g. lockscreen photo, scratches, serial number, keychain design) before handing it over.

3. Never send money upfront

Findora is 100% free. Never transfer "courier fees" or rewards to strangers through Easypaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer in advance.
