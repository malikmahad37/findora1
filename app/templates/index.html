Safety Guidelines for Handing Over Belongings

Please follow these common-sense rules for a smooth and safe handover

1
Meet in busy public places

Choose populated locations during daylight: campus library counters, security gates, metro stations, or cafes. Avoid private homes or secluded areas.

2
Verify secret ownership clues

Ask the claimant for a detail only the real owner knows (e.g. lockscreen wallpaper, scratches, sticker, inner pocket contents) before giving the item back.

3
Never pay advance fees

Findora is completely free. Beware of anyone asking for advance courier fees, delivery charges, or ransoms via Easypaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about reporting and recovering belongings

What should I do if I find someone's lost item?
Click Report a Found Item, upload a photo, and specify the city and campus or area where you found it. Keep at least one unique detail (like a sticker, lockscreen photo, or inner mark) private so you can verify the rightful owner when they message you.
How do I verify that a claimant is the real owner?
Ask them through Findora's direct messaging to describe specific details that weren't visible in your public post. For phones, ask them to unlock the device in front of you. For wallets or ID cards, ask for their full name matching the cards inside.
Is Findora completely free?
Yes, Findora is 100% free for everyone. There are no fees for reporting, browsing, or claiming items. We will never ask you for payment, and you should never pay anyone upfront to get your belonging returned.
Can I report an item lost on my university campus?
Yes! Select your city and province, and in the Area / Specific Spot field, write your university name and department (e.g., FAST NUCES Library, NUST H-12 Cafeteria, Punjab University Gate 2).
