Safety Guidelines for Handing Over Items

Follow these practical precautions when returning or claiming personal belongings:

1. Meet in busy public places

Choose locations with people around: campus library desks, department offices, metro stations, or well-lit cafes. Avoid meeting in secluded areas.

2. Check private verification details

Ask the claimant to confirm a hidden detail (lockscreen picture, sticker, scratch, or wallet card name) before handing over the item.

3. Never transfer money in advance

Findora is completely free. Beware of anyone requesting advance "shipping fees", "delivery charges", or rewards via Easypaisa or bank transfer.
